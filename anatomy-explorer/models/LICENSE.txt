Changes: meshes extracted, re-oriented, classified, simplified (two levels of detail), merged into
chunks, quantized and meshopt-compressed; a subcutaneous fat layer and depth-layer assignments were
derived; part descriptions were added. Generated by scripts/pipeline (see scripts/pipeline/README.md).

October 2026, for a general audience: the external genitalia were replaced by a smooth surface fitted to
the surrounding skin (the "urogenital region" skin and fat parts now hold that surface); pubic hair, the
penis, testes and epididymides and the arteries and veins of the penis were removed; vessels, ducts and
nerves that ran into them were trimmed where they met the new surface.
