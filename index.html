Earth Through Time

An interactive 3D globe of the whole history of the planet. Drag the timeline and watch continents drift, oceans open and ice sheets come and go.

Open Earth Through Time Needs WebGL2 · best on a laptop

The maps come from published plate reconstructions, so the coastlines at each age follow the science. Each moment is labelled as well constrained, modelled or speculative, so you can tell evidence from estimate.

Things to try

  • Press Tour for “The story of Earth” in 16 narrated stops, or “The human journey” in 12.
  • Open My place, pick Mumbai or any city, and follow it as its plate moves back through time.
  • Use Compare to put Pangaea and today side by side with a sliding divider.
  • Take a Deep Dive to the ground and walk, fly or swim among animals of that era.

Anatomy Explorer

A 3D human body you can take apart. Peel it layer by layer, pull regions apart, and find any structure by its medical name or the everyday word for it.

Open Anatomy Explorer Touch, or mouse and keyboard

Every body region comes in two levels of detail, so a light version appears first and sharper meshes follow. The same controls work with touch on a phone or tablet.

Things to try

  • Press 1 to 6, or use the Depth panel, to peel from skin down to organs.
  • Turn on Decouple and pinch or scroll over a region to pull its layers apart.
  • Search with everyday words like “kneecap”, “six pack” or “funny bone”.
  • Switch the body type to female, child, elderly, athletic or higher body fat.
